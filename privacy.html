PRISM

Privacy Policy

Effective September 26, 2026

PRISM is a running app for race predictions, training load, and PR tracking. This page explains what it stores, where, and who can see it. The short version: PRISM keeps what it needs to work, shows it only to the people you choose, and never sells it, uses it for ads, or tracks you across other apps.

Apple Health

If you allow it, PRISM reads your workouts, heart rate, and date of birth from Apple Health. It never writes to Apple Health.

  • Heart rate and date of birth stay on your iPhone. They are used on the device to calculate training load and age grading and are never uploaded.
  • Two summaries do leave the device: your training status (for example "productive", with its load ratio), which your teammates and coaches can see, and your weekly mileage totals, uploaded only if you turn on "Share weekly miles with my teams" in Settings.
  • Health data is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared with data brokers.

What your account stores

  • Account: your email address, first and last name, display name, and @username.
  • Running: races you log or import (name, distance, time, date), goal races and their prediction history, and your training status.
  • Social: friends, teams you join or coach, team workouts and completions, and cheers and comments on activities.
  • Notifications: a record of activity that concerns you (who cheered or commented on your races, friend requests, workouts from your coach) and whether you've seen it; which notifications you want; and, if you allow notifications, your device's push token.

Some settings (widget choices, age-grading details, onboarding progress) and your shoes are kept only on your device.

Who can see it

  • Friends you accept see your race activity, cheers, and comments in their feed.
  • Teammates and coaches see your team membership, training status, workout completions, and (if you opt in) weekly mileage. Goal races are private to you — coaches do not see them.
  • Other people can find you by your exact @username or email address in order to send a friend request.

Where it is stored

Account data is stored with Supabase, our database and sign-in provider, and is sent over encrypted connections. Access rules on the database limit each record to you and the people listed above. PRISM contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking software.

Push notifications are delivered by Apple Push Notification service, so the text of a notification (for example, the name of the friend who commented and the first part of their comment) passes through Apple to reach your device. Your weekly recap, streak and run reminders are scheduled on your device and never leave it.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time in the app: Settings → your profile → Delete Account. This immediately and permanently deletes your account and everything listed above, including any team you coach. Signing out removes your races and goal races from the device. Your data in Apple Health is never affected.

Children

PRISM is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.
